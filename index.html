MS - Brazil

Mato Grosso do Sul (MS) is a state in the Central-West region of Brazil. It is known for its beautiful natural landscapes, especially the Pantanal, one of the largest wetlands in the world. The state has a strong economy based on agriculture and cattle farming, and its capital, Campo Grande, is known for its green areas and cultural diversity.
